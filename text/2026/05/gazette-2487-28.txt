Niranjala Sandamali Sovisa the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate a candidate of that ward Number 18 - Kaludewala of Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Ms. I. A. Irangani Perera to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Wannakuwatta Waduge Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer of the Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ms. I. A. Irangani Perera was elected as the member of the of ward Number 18 - Kaludewala of the Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha. Kalindra Jayaweera Fernando, Returning Officer, Panadura Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Kalutara, 06th May, 2026.
