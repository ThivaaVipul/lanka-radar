(a) Number of vehicles, (b) Vehicle identification Number, (c) Description of the vehicle, (d) Technical specifications of the vehicle. Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development. Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, Colombo 01, May 17, 2026.
