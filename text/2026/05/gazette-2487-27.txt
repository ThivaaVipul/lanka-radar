Ragunathan, Returning Officer of the Seethawakapura Urban Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mr. Ubeysinghe Arachchilage Anura Ubeysinghe was elected as the member of the Seethawakapura Urban Council. P. Ragunathan, Returning Officer, Seethawakapura Urban Council. District Elections Office, Colombo, 04th May, 2026.
