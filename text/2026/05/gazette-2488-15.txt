Grade IV : Laying our men, Laying out women, Packers, Polythene/Cellophane bags and Cardboard Box Makers. Unskilled Workers and Stores Labourers. Grade V : Learners and Apprentices. The Learners and Apprentices referred above shall be deemed to be under training for at-least 156 working days.
