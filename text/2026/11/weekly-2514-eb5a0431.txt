Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education. 11-102 __________________________________ SRI LANKA RAILWAY DEPARTMENT Recruitment to the Training Period for Appointment on Open Basis to Grade III of the Posts of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) under the Scheme of Recruitment (MT -02) for the Service Category of Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 of the Railway Department - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from qualified citizens of Sri Lanka for the Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to the Training Period for Appointment on open basis to Grade III Posts of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) in the Railway Department, in accordance with the Scheme of Recruitment (MT-02) for the Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 Service Category of the Railway Department approved by the Public Service Commission on 15.06.2017 and subsequent amendments thereto. The closing date for receipt of applications is 07.12.2026 2. Recruitment through this open competitive examination will be made for 80 vacancies in the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) - (MT-02) under the Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 Service Category. Note - 1 Recruitment to the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) will be made for the following fields, in accordance with the course followed by the applicant: • General Fitter • Diesel Fitter • Mechanical Fitter • Machinist • Welder • Gas Cutter • Plater • Boilermaker / Boiler Welder • Motor Mechanic 3. (O/L) Examination in six (06) subjects at one sitting with Credit passes for Sinhala / Tamil / English Language, Mathematics, and two (02) other subjects. AND b. Having passed all subjects (excluding Common General Test) under the Physical Science / Technology stream at the G.C.E. Procedure of Recruitment Applicants fulfilling the qualifications specified in the notification will be subjected to a written competitive examination and an eligibility assessment interview. Recruitment to a one-year training period will be made initially based on the aggregate marks secured at the written competitive examination and the eligibility assessment interview, in order of merit and subject to the availability of vacancies. In the event of candidates scoring equal marks exceeding the number of vacancies, decisions regarding filling such vacancies shall be governed in accordance with the orders of the Public Service Commission pursuant to Section 92 of the Procedural Rules Code. Upon successful completion of the training, candidates shall be subjected to a written and practical examination and those who pass will be appointed to the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) - Grade III.
