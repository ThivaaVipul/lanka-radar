Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education. 11-102 __________________________________ SRI LANKA RAILWAY DEPARTMENT Recruitment to the Training Period for Appointment on Open Basis to Grade III of the Posts of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) under the Scheme of Recruitment (MT -02) for the Service Category of Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 of the Railway Department - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from qualified citizens of Sri Lanka for the Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to the Training Period for Appointment on open basis to Grade III Posts of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) in the Railway Department, in accordance with the Scheme of Recruitment (MT-02) for the Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 Service Category of the Railway Department approved by the Public Service Commission on 15.06.2017 and subsequent amendments thereto. The closing date for receipt of applications is 07.12.2026 2. Recruitment through this open competitive examination will be made for 80 vacancies in the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) - (MT-02) under the Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 Service Category. Note - 1 Recruitment to the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) will be made for the following fields, in accordance with the course followed by the applicant: • General Fitter • Diesel Fitter • Mechanical Fitter • Machinist • Welder • Gas Cutter • Plater • Boilermaker / Boiler Welder • Motor Mechanic 3. (O/L) Examination in six (06) subjects at one sitting with Credit passes for Sinhala / Tamil / English Language, Mathematics, and two (02) other subjects. AND b. Having passed all subjects (excluding Common General Test) under the Physical Science / Technology stream at the G.C.E. Procedure of Recruitment Applicants fulfilling the qualifications specified in the notification will be subjected to a written competitive examination and an eligibility assessment interview. Recruitment to a one-year training period will be made initially based on the aggregate marks secured at the written competitive examination and the eligibility assessment interview, in order of merit and subject to the availability of vacancies. In the event of candidates scoring equal marks exceeding the number of vacancies, decisions regarding filling such vacancies shall be governed in accordance with the orders of the Public Service Commission pursuant to Section 92 of the Procedural Rules Code. Upon successful completion of the training, candidates shall be subjected to a written and practical examination and those who pass will be appointed to the post of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) - Grade III. Method of Application (a) The application must strictly conform to the model format appended to this notification. It must be prepared on an A4 size (29.5 cm x 21 cm) paper utilizing both sides, and completed in the applicant's own handwriting. Applicants must ensure that headings 01 to 2.11 appear on the first page, and the remainder on subsequent pages. Computer-generated or typed applications may also be used. Applicants must verify whether the completed application conforms to the specimen form of application specified in the call notice. Applications not conforming to the specimen form of application or containing incomplete information will be rejected without notice. Therefore, prior to submission, re-verify that the application strictly conforms to the specimen form of application and that all details are accurately completed. (b) When preparing the application, it is essential to mention the name of the examination cited in the header, in English language as well, in applications prepared in both Sinhala and Tamil media. (c) The application should not be sent in the personal name of the General Manager of Railways or any other officer of the Railway Department. The top left corner of the envelope enclosing the application should clearly state: "Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to the Training Period for Appointment on Open Basis to the Posts of Railway Technical Management Assistant (Mechanical) Grade III under the Scheme of Recruitment- (MT-02) for the Management Assistant Technical Segment - 02 Service Category of the Railway Department - 2026" . (d) Applications must be forwarded by registered post to reach the following address on or before 07.12.2026 Applications received after that date will be rejected: General Manager of Railways, Office of the General Manager of Railways, P.O. Box 35, Olcott Mawatha, Colombo 10. (e) Officers currently employed in the Public Service or Provincial Public Service must submit their applications through the Head of the Department in which they are serving currently. 9. Admission to the Examination (a) This written examination will be conducted by an institution defined by the General Manager of Railways.
