(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 06.11.2026 MINISTRY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY Recruitment for the post of Lecturer (Grade II of Field/ Office-Based Officer Segment 01 Service Category) of the Sri Lanka Planetarium under the Ministry of Science and Technology on Open Basis - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from Sri Lankan citizens who have satisfied the qualifications specified in the notice to fill the existing vacancy for the post of Lecturer, Grade II of Field/ Office-Based Officer Segment 01 Service Category of the Sri Lanka Planetarium under the Ministry of Science and Technology. 1. Number of Vacancies - One (01) 2. Necessary Qualifications : (i) Educational Qualifications (a) A degree awarded by a foreign or state university recognized by the University Grants Commission or, (b) A degree awarded by a university established by an Act of the Parliament of Sri Lanka; or (c) A degree recognized under Section 25A of the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978. (ii) Experience : One (01) year of relevant experience in a position such as Lecturer/ Instructor, Demonstrator. Note : Self-certified copies of documents confirming the basic qualifications must be attached to the application. If the certificates submitted at the interview do not specify the relevant time periods. It is the responsibility of the applicant to obtain written confirmation of the relevant periods from the respective institutions and submit such confirmation. Certificates that do not specify the relevant period and for which the period has not been officially confirmed will not be considered for the award of marks. (iii) Age Limit Applicants must be not less than 21 years of age and not more than 35 years of age as at the closing date for the receipt of applications. (iv) Salary Scale In accordance with the provisions of Public Administration Circular No. 10/2025 dated 25.03.2025, the monthly salary scale applicable to this appointment is Rs. 62,230 - 10 x 1,190 - 11x1,360-15x1,670-114,140 (MN 06-2025). (v) Physical eligibility Every applicant must be physically and mentally fit to serve in any part of Sri Lanka and to perform the duties of the post. (vi) Other (a) Must be a citizen of Sri Lanka. (b) Applicants must possess an excellent character (This must be verified by a DS 04 certificate obtained from the Grama Niladhari of the applicant's place of residence within the last 06 months, along with another character certificate.) (c) Applicants must have fulfilled all the qualifications required for recruitment to the post in every respect by the date specified in the notice calling for applications/ Gazette notification. 3. Terms of Employment and Terms of Service : (i) This is a permanent and pensionable post. You will be required to contribute to the Widows' and Orphans' Pension Scheme/ Widowers' and Orphans' Pension Scheme, as applicable. You must make the prescribed contributions in accordance with the regulations issued by the Government from time to time. (ii) This appointment is subject to a probationary period of three (03) years. The officer must pass the First Efficiency Bar Examination, as specified in the Scheme of Recruitment, within three (03) years from the date of recruitment to the post. (iii) In accordance with the provisions of Public Administration Circular No. 18/2020 dated 16.10.2020, proficiency in prescribed other official language(s) must be obtained.
