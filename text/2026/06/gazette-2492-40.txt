And whereas the Secretary of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya, the recognized political party to which Mrs. Rajendran Adithyaya Subodhini the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)of the aforesaid Ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Kuliyapitiya Urban Council who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared Elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized Political party has communicated the decision to nominate Mrs. Kalyani Kathamuthu to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Harendra Bandara Rathnayaka, Returning Officer of the Kuliyapitiya Urban Council acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mrs. Kalyani Kathamuthu was elected as the member of the Kuliyapitiya Urban Council. R. M. H. B. Rathnayaka, Returning Officer, Kuliyapitiya Urban Council. District Election Office, Kurunegala. 09th June, 2026.
