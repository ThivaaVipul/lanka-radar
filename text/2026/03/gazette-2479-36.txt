(I) - GAZETTE EXTRAORDINARY OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 10.03.2026 Government of Sri Lanka Ministry of Energy Sri Lanka Electricity Industry Preliminary Transfer Plan Preamble The Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 36 of 2024 and subsequent amendments including Sri Lanka Electricity (Amendment) Act, No. 14 of 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the “Electricity Act”), require implementation of reforms to the electricity industry, to provide for the formulation of the national electricity policy as part of the national policy on energy. Fundamental objective of the Electricity Act is to ensure improved electricity industry performance through independent and accountable corporate entities responsible for the provision and maintenance, of a well-coordinated, efficient and economical system of continuous and reliable electricity supply throughout Sri Lanka at all times, through transparent policies. The Electricity Act facilitates the establishment of independent and accountable corporate entities for the efficient supply of electricity throughout the country, protects the interests of the consumers and facilitates the establishment and functioning of the unbundled electricity sector with the vision of developing the National Electricity Market. The transition, transfer and reorganization of the electricity industry in Sri Lanka will be guided by the Transfer Plan with detailed plans, proposals and strategies for the unbundling of the activities of the Ceylon Electricity Board (hereinafter referred to as the “CEB”) relating to the generation, transmission, distribution and supply of electricity. The Transfer Plan has two parts: the Preliminary Transfer Plan and the Final Transfer Plan. The Preliminary Transfer Plan contains all details of the vesting of the activities relating to the restructuring of the generation, transmission, distribution and supply of electricity as well as a financial plan for the vesting of assets and liabilities which shall be initially required for the successor companies to commence operations. The Final Transfer Plan comprises the process of implementation of all remaining matters required to complete the restructuring of the electricity industry. This Preliminary Transfer Plan comprises, required by subsection (3) of section 18 of the Electricity Act, including but not limited to details of the vesting of the activities of the CEB relating to the restructuring of the electricity generation based on hydro, thermal and renewable energy sources, electricity transmission services and maintenance of the National Grid of Sri Lanka, electricity demand forecasting and planning, procurement of electricity and sale in bulk form, economic dispatch of electricity, the management, including operation and maintenance of the System Control Centre and National Grid of Sri Lanka operation including transmission network expansion planning, electricity distribution and supply. The Preliminary Transfer Plan introduces the successor companies incorporated to take over the duties, functions, assets and liabilities of the CEB. Further it describes the mechanism of transfer and assignation of divisions, branches, units, functions and employees of the CEB to the successor companies. The Preliminary Transfer Plan also provides the provisional financial statements with vesting of assets and liabilities initially required for the successor companies to commence operations. Title This plan shall be cited as the “Sri Lanka Electricity Industry Preliminary Transfer Plan” (hereinafter referred to as the “Preliminary Transfer Plan”). 2. Reference The Preliminary Transfer Plan has been prepared by the Power Sector Reforms Secretariat (hereinafter referred to as the “PSRS”) established under section 38 of the Electricity Act according to the policy directions of the Minister assigned the subject of electricity (hereinafter referred to as the “Minister”), in consultation with the Minister assigned the subject of Finance and the Secretary to the Treasury. The technical sections of the Preliminary Transfer Plan, including allocation of existing cost centers and branches to the successor companies, registers of assets, contracts, contractual commitments, liabilities, licenses, approvals, permits and clearances of the CEB have been prepared in consultation with the General Manager and Additional General Managers of the CEB and the Board of Directors of the successor companies. The financial plan as part of the Preliminary Transfer Plan for the vesting of assets and liabilities which shall be initially required for the successor companies to commence operations has been prepared in consultation with the Finance Managers and Additional Finance Managers of the CEB. The technical schedules of the Preliminary Transfer Plan have been prepared in consultation with the relevant officers of the CEB who function as members of Steering Committees established by the Secretary to the Ministry of Energy to facilitate the PSRS in the reforms and restructuring of the CEB. The initial draft of the Preliminary Transfer Plan was shared with the employees of the CEB for their comments and observations. 3.
