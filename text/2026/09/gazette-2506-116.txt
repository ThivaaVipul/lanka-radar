(1) These rules shall apply to any person who directly or indirectly contravenes any provision of the Act or any rule, direction or decision made or issued thereunder. (2) These rules shall not apply to any employee of the Central Bank who is governed by the provisions of any rule, direction or decision made or issued under the Act relating to disciplinary control of such employees. 3. Where any Head of Department of the Central Bank (hereinafter referred to as the “Head of Department”) has reasonable grounds to believe that a person has directly or indirectly contravened any provision of the Act, or any rule, direction or decision made or issued thereunder, such Head of Department shall, as soon as practicable, cause an examination, investigation or inquiry, as the case may be, to be conducted into such matter. 4. (1) Where, pursuant to the examination, investigation or inquiry conducted under rule 3, any direct or indirect contravention of any provision of the Act, or any rule, direction or decision made or issued thereunder, is prima facie identified, the Head of Department shall issue a notice in writing to the person who is alleged to have committed such contravention, informing such person of such contravention, and requiring such person to show cause, within such period as specified in such notice, as to why such person shall not be held to have committed such contravention. (2) (a) The notice shall set out - (i) the findings of the examination, investigation or inquiry, as the case may be, pertaining to the alleged contravention; and (ii) the time period within which such person is required to show cause, which shall not be less than fourteen days from the date of the notice. (b) Such notice may, inter alia, require such person to provide information on, or respond to, any specific question raised therein relating to the alleged contravention. (3) Upon receipt of the notice, such person shall respond to such notice within the period specified therein, providing all material facts or information to substantiate as to why such person shall not be held to have committed such contravention. (4) Any extension of the time specified in the notice shall not be granted to such person to respond to the notice, unless the Head of Department is satisfied that there are reasonable grounds to do so. (5) Any person who fails to respond to the notice issued under this rule shall be deemed to have accepted the liability of the contravention specified therein. 5. (1) The Head of Department shall, without undue delay, consider the response received by him pursuant to sub-rule (3) of rule 4. In considering such response, he shall take the following factors into account: - (a) the severity of the alleged contravention and whether such contravention is recurring; (b) whether any loss or damage is caused to the depositors or any other person due to such contravention; (c) whether the person alleged to have committed such contravention is unduly benefitted from such contravention; (d) the financial resources of the person alleged to have committed the contravention; (e) any mitigating factors; and (f) such other matter as the Head of Department considers to be relevant. (2) The following factors may be considered when issuing a letter of reprimanding or severe reprimanding: - (a) the severity or recurrence or continuation of the contravention; (b) the position held by the person who committed such contravention; (c) the systemic importance of the financial institution, where applicable; and (d) any other relevant factor as may be considered to be relevant by the Committee or the Governing Board, as the case may be, based on the circumstances pertaining to each such matter. 12. (1) An administrative fine referred to in rules 6 and 7 shall be- (a) in the case of an administrative fine imposed by the Committee under rule 6, an amount not exceeding Rupees five million; (b) in the case of an administrative fine imposed by the Governing Board under rule 7, an amount not exceeding two-thirds of the maximum fine specified in section 111 of the Act. (2) The Central Bank may, if it considers such publication necessary, publish the information relating to administrative fines imposed by the Committee or the Governing Board under these rules in such form and manner as may be decided by the Central Bank. (3) All administrative fines shall be payable to the special account maintained for the purpose at the Finance Department of the Central Bank. 13. (1) Where the person who is found to have committed the contravention is a director, the chief executive officer or an officer performing executive functions of a financial institution, a copy of the communication referred to in rule 8 shall also be sent to the Chairman of the Board of Directors of such financial institution. (2) Where the person who is found to have committed the contravention is the Chairman of the Board of Directors of a financial institution, a copy of the communication referred to in rule 8 shall also be sent to the Secretary to the Board of Directors of such financial institution, directing such Secretary to place such communication before the Board of Directors at the meeting which will be held immediately after the date of receipt of such communication. 14. (1) A person aggrieved by any administrative measure taken by the Committee under rule 6 shall be entitled to make an appeal to the Governing Board, within a period of fourteen days from the date of receipt of the communication referred to in rule 8. (2) In considering an appeal made to it under sub-rule (1), where the Governing Board is of the view that the findings of the initial examination, investigation or inquiry conducted by the Head of Department are not adequate, the Governing Board may direct such Head of Department to further examine, investigate, or inquire into the matter or expand the scope of the examination, investigation or inquiry as the case may be, and submit an updated report, in respect of the alleged contravention. Any person aggrieved by- (a) an administrative measure taken by the Governing Board under rule 7; or (b) the decision of the Governing Board under rule 14, may make an appeal to the Court of Appeal against such measure or decision within a period of fourteen days from the date of receipt of the communication thereof. 16. (1) For the purpose of imposition of administrative measures under these rules, the Governing Board shall appoint a Committee of three members consisting of one Deputy Governor and two Assistant Governors of the Central Bank. (2) The Deputy Governor appointed to the Committee shall be the Chairperson of the Committee. The Chairperson shall appoint an officer of the Central Bank not below the rank of a Deputy Director as the Secretary to the Committee. 17. Nothing contained in these rules shall be read and construed as applying to, or in any manner affecting, the general activities carried out by the Central Bank under any other written law administered by it in the ordinary course of business. 18. For the purpose of these rules- “Act” means the Central Bank of Sri Lanka Act, No. 16 of 2023; “Central Bank” means the Central Bank of Sri Lanka established under the provisions of the Act; “financial institution” shall have the same meaning as in the Act; “Governing Board” means the Governing Board of the Central Bank established under section 8 of the Act; “Head of Department” means any officer appointed or designated by the Central Bank who is responsible for the management, supervision and administration of any department of the Central Bank, or any officer authorized to act in such capacity; and “person” shall have the same meaning as in the Act.
