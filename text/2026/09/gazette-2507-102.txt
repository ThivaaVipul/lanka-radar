Ashaful Hawfu Isra the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of that Thambalagamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of Thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Mrs. Sahabdeen Natheera Umma to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Selladhore Kaliuganathan Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer of the Thambalagamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mrs. Sahabdeen Natheera Umma was elected as the member of the Thambalagamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha. S. K. Deva Neranjan, Returning Officer, Thambalagamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Trincomalee, 25th September, 2026.
