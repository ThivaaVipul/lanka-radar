(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 25.09.2026 Post - Vacant COMMISSION TO INVESTIGATE ALLEGATIONS OF BRIBERY OR CORRUPTION Inviting Applications for Recruitment to the Post of Driver (Grade III) on Open Basis - 2026 IN accordance with the provisions set out in Section 26(1) of the Anti-Corruption Act, No. 9 of 2023, applications are invited from citizens of Sri Lanka who satisfy the qualifications specified in this notification to be recruited on an open basis for 46 vacancies in the post of Driver (Grade III) -Primary Level (Skilled) category of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption. 1. Qualifications required :- 1.1 Educational qualifications : Should have passed six subjects at the G.C.E. (Ordinary Level) examination in not more than two sittings with credit passes for two subjects, including Sinhala/ Tamil Language. 1.2 Professional qualifications : Should possess a driving license for heavy vehicles issued by the Commissioner General of the Department of Motor Traffic. 1.3 Experience : Should have three years of experience (continuous/discontinuous) as a driver. (Should prove the experience through documentary evidence.) 1.4 Physical fitness : Every candidate must be in good physical and mental fitness to serve in any part of Sri Lanka and to perform the duties of the post. Must have good eyesight both day and night, and it should be certified by a medical report. Height should be 5 feet or above. 1.5 Other qualifications : i. Should be a citizen of Sri Lanka. ii. Should be of excellent moral character. iii. All qualifications required for recruitment to the post should have been fulfilled in every aspect as at the date specified in the notification for calling of applications. 2. Age :- - Minimum limit - 25 years - Maximum limit - 45 years 3. Salary Scale :- Rs. 43,280-10x490-10x540-10x590-12x630 - Rs. 67,040 Accordingly, the salary code applicable to this post is AC-PL-3-2025, and the initial salary step will be Rs. 43,280/-. In addition, other allowances applicable to the post, as approved by Parliament on 17.03.2026, will be paid. (If recruitment is made before 01.01.2027, salaries will be paid in accordance with Schedule II, i.e., the salary will be Rs. 40,834.00). 4. Method of Recruitment :- 100% on an open basis. 4.1 Consists of a professional examination and a structured interview.
