C. M. Abdul Asis the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Negombo Municipal Council who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared Elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Mr. M. I. Mohamad Israth to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Withana Appuhamillage Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer of the Negombo Municipal Council acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mr. M. I. Mohamad Israth was elected as the member of the Negombo Municipal Council, W. A. Raviendra Wickramasinghe, Returning Officer, Negombo Municipal Council. District Election Office, Gampaha, 22nd of September, 2026. EOG 09 – 0448 1A - PG 8016 - 04 (09/2026) PRINTED AT THE DEPARTMENT OF GOVERNMENT PRINTING, SRI LANKA.
