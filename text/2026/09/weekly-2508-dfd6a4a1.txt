The application form is available through the "Online Applications - Recruitment/ EB Exams" Section under "Our Service" on the website www.doenets.lk of the Department of Examinations and applications can be submitted online only. After submitting the application online, it should be downloaded, fill in the relevant Sections of the printed copy in handwriting, attest the applicant's signature, and submit a copy of the application to the Head of the Institution for inclusion in his/her personal life.
