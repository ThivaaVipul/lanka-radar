Date : .............................................................................. 09-342 ________________________________ PUBLIC SERVICES COMMISSION Recruitment under Open Stream to the Post of Legal Officer in Grade III of Executive Service Category at the Department of Agrarian Development under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation - 2026 ON the order of the Public Services Commission, applications are hereby called from citizens of Sri Lanka, who have possessed following qualifications, for filling of 02 post of Legal Officer in Grade III of executive service category at the Department of Agrarian Development, which has fallen vacant, under open stream. (a) The closing date for application is 30/10/2026 1. Functions assigned to the post: - Providing legal consultations to the District Offices, Agrarian Development Centres fallen under the Department of Agrarian Development and its scope, preparing legal documents such as draft bills, regulations, observation reports, proxies etc . - Maintaining coordination with the Department of Attorney General, taking action on complaints on human rights violations, and providing consultation regarding the court cases, of which the officers Department of Agrarian Development and in above mentioned public institutions fallen under the scope of the Department are named as respondents on matters relevant to the scope, and appearing before the court on such cases. - Providing consultations to take action as per Section 4,5,6 of the Chapter XXXIII and Section I of Chapter XI of the Establishments Code, providing necessary guidance, and carrying out other relevant activities and performing other duties entrusted by the Commissioner General of Agrarian Development. 2. Method of Recruitment: Out of the candidates, who have satisfied qualifications stipulated in the notification, the candidates who secure highest marks on the results of an interview to be held to evaluate the eligibility by an interview board appointed by the Public Services Commission will be recruited to the post fallen vacant. The interview will be held conforming to the marking scheme (Mentioned under No. 7) approved by the Public Services Commission.
