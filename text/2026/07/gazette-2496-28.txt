Further directs, no importer, packer, distributor, transporter, store owner or trader shall import, pack, distribute, transport, store, or sell or display for sale, expose for sale or offer for sale, wholesale or retail any items of imported products mentioned covered by the below specifications unless it has been inspected and approved for sale under the Import Inspection Scheme operated by the Sri Lanka Standards Institution under the Imports (Standardization and Quality Control) Regulation 2006 made under Section 20 of the Imports and Exports (Control) Act, No. 01 of 1969, as amended by Act, No. 48 of 1985 and Act, No. 28 of 1987. (i) Sri Lanka Standard Specification for Single use sanitary towels (SLS 1732) (ii) Sri Lanka Standard Specification for single-use ultra-thin/slim sanitary towels (SLS 1748) This directive is subject to all future amendments made by the Sri Lanka Standards Institution to the above Standards.
