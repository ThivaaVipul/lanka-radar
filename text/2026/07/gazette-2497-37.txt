Anura Karunathilake, Minister of Energy. Colombo, 13th July, 2026. Regulations 1. These regulations may be cited as the Petroleum Resources Agreement Regulations No. 01 of 2026 2. Any person who is desirous of exploring or developing petroleum resources within one or more identified blocks shall submit an investment proposal (in these regulations hereinafter referred to as the "bid"), together with the payment of an administrative fee, specified in the relevant call for bids. 3. The bid referred to in regulation 2, shall include the accurate information in the similar format as specified in the dossier for the call for bids. The bids submitted under regulation 2, shall be evaluated by the Project Committee appointed by the Secretary to the Treasury on the request made by the line Ministry, in accordance with the criteria specified in the corresponding call for bids. 5. The Negotiating Committee and the Authority shall with the assistance of the Project Committee, where necessary, negotiate with contractors the terms and conditions to be incorporated into each Petroleum Resources Agreement based on the relevant Model Petroleum Resources Agreement. 7. The Authority shall forward the finalized Petroleum Resources Agreement, to the Minister and the Minister shall submit the same to the Cabinet of Ministers for approval.
