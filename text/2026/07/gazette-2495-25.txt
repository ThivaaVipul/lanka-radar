By virtue of the power vested in terms of Section 66(2) of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023, the Central Bank of Sri Lanka has cancelled the licence issued to Nation Lanka Finance PLC under the Finance Business Act, No. 42 of 2011 with effect from 03 July 2026. Accordingly, Nation Lanka Finance PLC is not permitted to carry on finance business with effect from 03 July 2026. Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chairman of the Governing Board and Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.
