W. D. N. R. Rodrigo, who was appointed as the Administrator of Nation Lanka Finance PLC by Order published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/57 dated 04.07.2025, effective from 04.07.2025, and subsequently extended till 03.07.2026, is hereby further extended for a period of six (06) months, commencing from 04.07.2026 and ending on 03.01.2027. This extension is made to facilitate the continuation of administrative and statutory functions of the Administrator for Nation Lanka Finance PLC in accordance with the provisions of the Banking (Special Provisions) Act, No. 17 of 2023. Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chairman of the Governing Board and Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.
