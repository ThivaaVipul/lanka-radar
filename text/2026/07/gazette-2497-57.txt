Sinnarasa Rajeepa the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha, who belongs to such Party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared Elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Miss. Kirushnasami Dorin to fill the said vacancy; Therefore, I, Rajendra Saseelan, Returning Officer of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Miss. Kirushnasami Dorin was elected as the member of the Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha , R. Saseelan, Returning Officer, Valikamam South Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Jaffna, 17th of July, 2026.
