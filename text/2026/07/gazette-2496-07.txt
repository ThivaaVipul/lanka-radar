Bulathsinhalage Don Malsha Anne Sithumini the member who belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1)(a) of the aforesaid Ordinance, to nominate a candidate of that Ward Number 06 Thalavila of Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated his decision to nominate Mrs. Weerasinghe Jayawardhana Shanika Lakmali to fill such vacancy; Therefore I, Siriniwasage Lakshitha Ruwan Jayanayake, Returning Officer of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha, acting in terms of Section 66A(l)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262), do hereby declare that Mrs. Weerasinghe Jayawardhana Shanika Lakmali was elected as the member of the Ward Number 06 Thalavila of the Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. S. L. R. Jayanayake, Returning Officer, Kalpitiya Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Puttalam, 03rd July, 2026.
