PART I MONEY LAUNDERING, TERRORIST FINANCING AND FINANCING OF PROLIFERATION OF WEAPONS OF MASS DESTRUCTION RISK MANAGEMENT 3. Every DNFBP shall take measures specified in these rules for the purpose of identifying, assessing and understanding its money laundering, terrorist financing and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction risks (hereinafter referred to as the “risk”) associated with its customers, the countries or regions in which those customers are engaged in business, its products, services, transactions and delivery channels by conducting customer due diligence (hereinafter referred to as the "CDD") and apply a risk-based approach for allocating resources, monitoring, managing and mitigating such risk. (1) Every DNFBP shall provide a timely report of the effectiveness of the risk controls and mitigation measures based on the findings of its risk assessment and regular review mechanisms to its Board of Directors or senior management. Internal Controls, Policies, Procedures, Compliance, Audit and Training 12. (1) Every DNFBP shall formulate an internal policy approved by its Board of Directors or senior management subject to the written laws in force for the time being, on anti-money laundering, countering the financing of terrorism and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. Such policy shall be reviewed annually or sooner by the DNFBP, whenever there are changes to the risk or such written laws, and the approval for such reviewed policy shall be obtained from its Board of Directors or senior management. (2) The detailed procedures, controls and systems shall be developed by each DNFBP in compliance with such policy and be approved by the Board of Directors or senior management. (3) Such policy, procedures, controls and systems shall include but not limited to, risk assessment procedures, CDD measures, requirement of record retention, handling of attempted transactions, reliance on third parties, compliance with targeted financial sanctions, anti-money laundering, countering the financing of terrorism and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction audits, the detection and internal reporting procedure of unusual or suspicious transactions, the obligation to report suspicious transactions in terms of section 7 and transactions specified under section 6 of the Act to the Financial Intelligence Unit, transaction monitoring, handling of breaches, escalation mechanism, whistleblowing, testing, new product, service, delivery channel or technology approvals, avoidance of tipping-off and confidentially obligations and termination of business or professional relationship. 13.Every DNFBP shall in formulating policies, procedures, systems, and controls take into consideration the risk that may arise from the use of new or developing technologies, including those having features of anonymity and inconsistency with the CDD measures specified in Part II of these rules. 14.Every DNFBP shall - (a) appoint a compliance officer in terms of the provisions of the Act, and inform forthwith the Financial Intelligence Unit on the appointment and any changes to the appointment of such compliance officer; (d) develop and implement a comprehensive employee and agent due diligence and screening procedure to be carried out at the time of appointing, entering into a contract or any agreement or hiring of employees whether permanent, contractual or outsourced or agents; Branches, Majority-owned Subsidiaries and Group of companies 15. Every group of companies consisting of any DNFBP and any other entity shall, subject to the provisions of the Act and these rules, implement group-wide programmes which shall be applicable and appropriate for all branches and majority-owned subsidiaries of the group with a view to combatting money laundering, terrorist financing and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction and shall include the following in addition to the measures referred to in rule 14:- (a) formulate policies, procedures, controls and systems for sharing information within the group of companies consisting of any DNFBP and any other entity for purposes of conducting CDD and risk management; (b) provide or receive information of customers, accounts, transactions, analysis of unusual transactions and underlying information of such transactions, typologies and of audits, to or from all branches and majority- owned subsidiaries of the group when necessary for implementing the anti-money laundering, countering the financing of terrorism and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction measures; (c) maintain adequate safeguards on the confidentiality and use of information exchanged within the group including safeguards in relation to divulging of information; and (d) ensure effective implementation of its anti-money laundering, countering the financing of terrorism and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction framework in branches and majority-owned subsidiaries and group of companies consisting of any DNFBP and any other entity. 16. (1) Every DNFBP shall ensure that its foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries and group of companies consisting of any DNFBP and any other entity apply measures which are consistent with the Act, regulations, rules, Orders and directions made or issued under the Act to combat money laundering, terrorist financing and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction, where the relevant written laws of the relevant foreign country provide less stringent requirements than those provided by the Act, regulations, rules, Orders and directions made or issued under the Act. (2) Where the relevant written laws of a foreign country do not permit the implementation of anti-money laundering, countering the financing of terrorism and financing of proliferation of weapons of mass destruction measures which are consistent with the Act, regulations, rules, Orders and directions made or issued under the Act, then the DNFBP shall apply appropriate additional measures to manage and mitigate the risk and inform the Financial Intelligence Unit and the relevant supervisory authority of the measures that have not been implemented, risk stemming therefrom and the additional measures implemented to address the risk. (3) For the purpose of paragraph (2), ‘additional measures’ shall include extra precautionary measures and increased oversight and controls. PART II CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) 18. (1) In terms of the provisions of subsection (3) section 2 of the Act, a DNFBP shall not open, operate or maintain an account, where the holder of such account cannot be identified, including any anonymous account or any account identified by number only, any account or any business or professional relationship which to the knowledge of the DNFBP is being operated under a fictitious or false name (hereinafter referred to as “Fictitious or Anonymous Account”). (2) A Fictitious or Anonymous Account includes an account where the ownership is transferrable without the knowledge of the DNFBP and accounts that are operated and maintained with the account holder’s name omitted. 19. Every DNFBP shall maintain information in such a manner that the records and information in relation to a given customer of such DNFBP can be readily retrievable. 20. A DNFBP shall not maintain information of transaction or professional relationship separate from the DNFBP’s standard operational processes, controls, systems or procedures. 21. Risk Based Due Diligence 25. (1) Without prejudice to the provisions of rule 22, where any customer is rated as a customer posing a low risk, a DNFBP may conduct, but not limited to, the following simplified CDD measures as such DNFBP may deem appropriate- (a) verifying the identity of the customer and the beneficial owner after the establishment of the business or professional relationship (delayed verification); (b) reducing the frequency of periodic reviews of customer relationship; (c) not collecting specific information or carrying out specific measures to understand the purpose and intended nature of the business or professional relationship, but inferring the purpose and nature from the type of transactions or business or professional relationship established. A DNFBP shall not, under any circumstances, establish a business or professional relationship or conduct any transaction with a customer prior to verifying the identity of such customer and beneficial owner, if any, where the risk level of such customer is assessed as high. Customer and Transaction Monitoring 30. Every DNFBP shall monitor all business or professional relationships on an ongoing basis to ensure that the transactions are consistent with the information obtained under these rules, the risk profile of such customer and the DNFBP’s knowledge of such customer. 31. (1) Every DNFBP shall obtain information and examine the background and purpose of all complex, unusually large transactions and all unusual patterns of transactions, which have no apparent economic or prima facie lawful purpose. (2) The background and purpose of such transactions shall be inquired into and findings shall be kept on record with a view to making such information available to the relevant competent authority, supervisory authority or the Financial Intelligence Unit when required to do so.
