Division (Sinhala Medium) 10-452 _________________________________ COMMISSION TO INVESTIGATE ALLEGATIONS OF BRIBERY OR CORRUPTION Calling For Applications For Open Recruitment To The Post of Additional Director-General Of The Commission To Investigate Allegations Of Bribery Or Corruption - 2026 IN terms of the provisions of Section 26(1) of the Anti-Corruption Act, No. 9 of 2023, applications are invited from citizens of Sri Lanka who satisfy the qualifications specified in this notification to be recruited on an open basis to the post of Additional Director-General belonging to the Senior Level service category of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (The number of vacancies is 01). 01 . Qualifications required : 1.1 Educational qualifications : A Postgraduate Degree in the field of Law from a University recognized by the University Grants Commission or from an institution recognized by the University Grants Commission as a Postgraduate Degree-awarding institution. 1.2 Professional qualifications : Must be an Attorney-at-Law of the Supreme Court of Sri Lanka. 1.3 Experience : Not less than 15 years of active professional experience in criminal litigation after being sworn in as an Attorney-at-Law of the Supreme Court of Sri Lanka. 1.4 Physical fitness : Every candidate must be in good physical and mental fitness to serve in any part of Sri Lanka and to perform the duties of the post. 1.5 Other qualifications : i. Must be a citizen of Sri Lanka ii. Must be of excellent moral character. iii. The qualifications required for recruitment to the post shall have been fulfilled in every aspect as at the closing date of applications. 02. Age Limit: - Minimum age - 40 years - Maximum age - 54 years 03. Salary Scale: - Rs. 212,000 -10x4,950-Rs. 261,500 Accordingly, the salary code applicable to this post is AC-E 4-2025, and the initial salary step will be Rs. 212,000 /-. In addition, other allowances applicable to the post will be paid at the rates approved by Parliament on 17.03.2026. (If recruited before 01.01.2027, salaries will be paid according to Schedule II of the salary scale, i.e., the salary will be Rs. 183,300/=).
