(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 30.10.2026 MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, HIGHER EDUCATION AND VOCATIONAL EDUCATION Department of Technical Education and Training REGISTRATION OF EXTERNAL RESOURCE PERSONS AS VISITING INSTRUCTORS FOR ACADEMIC YEAR 2027 APPLICATIONS are invited from suitably qualified persons for recruitment as visiting staff at Technical Colleges and Colleges of Technology under the Department of Technical Education and Training for the academic year 2027. Applications shall be submitted online through the official website of Department of Technical Education and th Training, www.dtet.gov.lk on or before 07 December 2026. 02. Educational and Professional qualifications required for visiting instructors: I. Recruitment of visiting instructors shall be in accordance with the qualifications specified in the circular approved by the Public Service Commission. Accordingly, all applicants should possess the required educational and professional qualifications relevant to the course/subject area before the closing date of applications. II. Educational and professional qualifications to be met by applicants for teaching or training in NVQ Level 3 / 4 courses a) NCT or NVQ Level 5 or equivalent government recognized qualification in the relevant field with minimum three (3) years working experience in teaching the relevant subject/ industry. b) NVQ Level 6 or equivalent government recognized qualification in the relevant field with minimum (3) years working experience in teaching the relevant subject/ industry. (eg. Other qualifications which are recognized by the government like HNDE/ NDT/ NDES.) c) Post Graduate Diploma / Degree qualification or equivalent government recognized qualification in the relevant field with minimum (3) years working experience in teaching the relevant subject/ industry. III. Educational and professional qualifications to be met by applicants for teaching or training in NVQ level 5 courses. a) NVQ Level 5 or equivalent government recognized qualification in the relevant field with minimum three (3) years working experience in teaching the relevant subject/Industry or ten (10) years experience in teaching /Industry with NCT. (Only for teaching in semester 1 ( first year ) and semester 2 (second year) ) b) NVQ Level 6 or equivalent government recognized qualification in the relevant field with minimum (3) years working experience in teaching the relevant subject/ industry. (eg. Other qualifications which are recognized by the government like HNDE/ NDT/ NDES). c) Post Graduate Diploma /Degree qualification or equivalent professional qualification with three (3) years experience in teaching in the relevant subject /Industry. d) Post Graduate Degree / Doctorate qualification or equivalent professional qualification with three (3) years experience in teaching in the relevant subject /Industry. The remuneration paid to External Resource Persons will be determined based on their academic qualifications, professional experience, and NVQ Level of the course, in accordance with the circulars on payments to Visiting Instructors issued by the Director General of the Department. 04. Submission of Applications :- Public officers shall download and print their duly submitted online applications and produce it at the interview, together with the recommendation from the Head of their respective institution confirming that the officer may be released to undertake the duties of the visiting appointment. 05. Selection Process: a. A pplicants will be selected for registration as visiting instructor only upon appearing for an interview convened by the Director/Principal of the relevant College of Technology/ Technical College. Priority will be given to candidates with prior teaching experience in the relevant subject areas. The appointment of any selected visiting instructor may be cancelled at any time by the Director/ Principal of the institute, upon written justification, if it is confirmed that the appointee: • is a person convicted of a criminal offence, or • has submitted fraudulent educational or professional qualifications, or • has been involved in anti-social activities, or • has been subject to student complaints indicating unsatisfactory teaching performance. b. Applicants over 65 years of age may be considered for available visiting positions only in the event that no suitably qualified applicants below 65 years of age have applied. c. If any applicant who has applied to serve as an visiting instructor has previously served as an visiting instructor at a COT/Technical College within the past year and has engaged in any disciplinary misconduct that could bring disrepute to the department or the institute, the Director/ Principal of the relevant institute shall have the discretion, based on the circumstances, to either reject the application without calling the applicant for an interview or to refrain from summoning such applicant for the interview. d. External resource persons registered as instructors are mandatorily required to participate in all examination-related duties of the relevant College of Technology /Technical College. This includes, but is not limited to, the preparation of question papers for semester-end/year-end/final examinations, conducting examinations, and marking answer scripts - whether or not additional remuneration is provided for such tasks. 06. This visiting registration is only for the academic year 2027 and does not entitle to any permanent position. 07. Where sufficient applications are not received, or no suitably qualified applicants are available to fill the approved vacancies and where vacancies arise during the academic year, the Director / Principal of the respective college may re-invite applications online as required. 08. If there any differences among Sinhala /Tamil /English publications Sinhala publication is enforced. D. Wathsala Marambage, Director General, Department of Technical Education & Training.
