Method of Recruitment :- Recruitments will be made on results of a written examination and a vocational test. First, a written examination will be held and from the candidates who pass the written examination, twice the number to be recruited will be called for the practical and vocational test in the order of merits of marks obtained by them. Candidates who pass the practical and vocational test, will be recruited in the order of merits of the aggregate marks of the written examination and vocational test.
