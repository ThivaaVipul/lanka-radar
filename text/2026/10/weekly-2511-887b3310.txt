(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 16.10.2026 DEPARTMENT OF ANIMAL PRODUCTION AND HEALTH Calling for applications for open/limited interview for Recruitment to the post of Laboratory Aide, Grade III of the Primary Grade Semi-Skilled Service Category - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from eligible applicants for the selection of suitable applicants for appointment to the 20 vacancies available in the post of Laboratory Aide, Grade III of the Primary Grade Semi-skilled Service Category, as per the approval No. PMO/RRPCM/2025/decision dated 12.11.2025 of the Officers' Committee appointed by the Cabinet to Review the Recruitment Process and Cadre Management in the Public Service. Applications prepared in accordance with the specimen application at the end of this notice should be sent by registered post to the address "Director General, Department of Animal Production and Health, P.O. Box 13, Getambe, Peradeniya" on or before the following date, certified by open applicants directly and by limited applicants by their Head of Division. The title "Post of Laboratory Aide, Department of Animal Production and Health, Sri Lanka" should be clearly mentioned in the top hand corner of the envelope. Limited applicants must have completed a minimum service period of five (05) years in a primary grade unskilled category post of the Department of Animal Production and Health, must have been confirmed in the post, and must possess the basic qualifications required for recruitment to the post. Closing date for calling applications is 13.11.2026 Note : Complaints regarding the loss or delay of an application or letter related to the same in the post will not be considered. The applicant himself should sustain all the losses that may cause due to the delay in forwarding the applications until the last day. Further, applications of applicants those who have not fulfilled the basic qualifications will be rejected without notice. 1. Method of Recruitment I. 50% of the vacancies in the posts of Laboratory Aide will be recruited on an open basis and 50% on a limited basis. II. The number of appointments and the date of effective date of appointments will be determined by the Director General of the Department of Animal Production and Health, as authorized by the Public Service Commission. 2. Terms of Engagement in Service : I. This post is permanent and pensionable. You are subject to policy decision taken by the government in the future in respect of the pension scheme you are entitled to. Further, you should contribute to the Widow's and Orphans/ Widowers' and Orphans Pension Scheme (W&OP) as specified by the government from time to time. II. Selected applicants will be appointed to fill vacancies of a post of Laboratory Aide in the Department of Animal Production and Health. III. The applicant appointed to the post should acquire the relevant level of competency in language as per the provisions and incidentals there to in the Public Administration Circular No. 18/2020 and dated 06.10.2020. IV. Every applicant is obliged to serve in any part of Sri Lanka. V. Other terms and conditions of service: This appointment is subject to the Scheme of Recruitment for the Primary Grade Semi-skilled Service Category of the Department of Animal Production and Health, approved by the Public Service Commission No. PSC/EST/5/1/6/1/2012 dated 27.09.2012 and the amendments made thereto from time to time, the Procedural Rules and the Public Service Commission the Establishments Code of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, Financial Regulations of the Government and other Departmental Orders. 3. Salary Scale : This post is entitled to monthly Salary Scale of Rs. 41,800 - 10 x 490 - 10 x 540 - 10x590 - 12 x 630 - Rs. 65,560 (PL 2-2025) as per schedule II of P.A.C. No. 10/2025 dated 25.03.2025. Salaries will be paid in terms of the Schedule III of the provisions of this circular. 4.
