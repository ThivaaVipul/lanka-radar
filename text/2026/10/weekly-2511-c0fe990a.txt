(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 16.10.2026 SRI LANKA FOREIGN SERVICE Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to Grade III of The Sri Lanka Foreign Service - 2026 THE notification published in Gazette No. 2505 dated 04 September, 2026, titled "Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to Grade III of the Sri Lanka Foreign Service - 2026", is hereby amended as follows: 1. The paragraph relating to Age under Item No. 04 of the above Gazette Notification shall be amended as follows: "The applicant should not be less than twenty-two (22) years of age and should not be more than thirty-five (35) years of age as at 06.10.2026. Accordingly, the age qualification shall be satisfied only if the applicant's date of birth falls on or before 06.10.2004 and on or after 06.10.1991. Candidates who are already in the Public Service, shall be forty (40) years, (Accordingly only those who are born on or after 06.10.1986 are eligible to apply) and should possess a satisfactory record of service. II. The closing date for the submission of application is hereby extended up to 30.10.2026. The acceptance of applications will open on 19th October, 2026 at 09.00 a.m. and will close on 30th October, 2026 at 09.00 p.m. All other provisions stipulated in the above Gazette Notification shall remain unchanged and in force, and the date for the verification of qualifications shall remain unchanged as 06.10.2026. By order of the Public Service Commission. Secretary, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism. Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism 16th October, 2026. 10-717 ________________________________ PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development OPEN COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION FOR RECRUITMENT TO GRADE III OF THE SRI LANKA INLAND REVENUE SERVICE - 2026 THE notification published in Gazette No. 2500 dated 31 July 2026 and Gazette No. 2,504 dated 28 August, 2026, titled "Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to Grade III of the Sri Lanka Inland Revenue Service - 2026" , is hereby amended as follows: I. The paragraph relating to age under Section 79cc) of the aforesaid Gazette Notification shall be amended as follows : The maximum age limit for persons serving in the Public Service or Provincial Public Service shall be 40 years. (Candidates serving in the Public Service should not have been subjected to disciplinary punishment in terms of Public Service Commission Circular No. 01/2020.) II. The closing date for the submission of applications is hereby extended up to 23 October, 2026. All other provisions of the aforesaid Gazette Notification shall remain unchanged, and the date for determining the eligibility of candidates shall remain unchanged as 31 August, 2026.
