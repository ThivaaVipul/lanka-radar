Wing), the recognized political party to which Ms. Siththaralingam Vidurshika the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Kotmale Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared Elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Ms. Arunachalam Umadevi to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Atugedara Soradhipathi Radha Subhashini, Returning Officer of the Kotmale Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Ms. Arunachalam Umadevi was elected as the member of the Kotmale Pradeshiya Sabha. Atugedara Soradhipathi Radha Subhashini, Returning Officer, Kotmale Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Nuwaraeliya, 30th of October, 2026.
