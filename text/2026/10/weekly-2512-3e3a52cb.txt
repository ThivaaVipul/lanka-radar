Note - the approval for these recruitments has been granted by letter No. DMS/CAD/EXAM/CM dated 26.06.2026 issued by Director General of the Department of Management Services. 01. The application form has been published on the website of the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka, www.doenets.lk, through "Online Applications - Recruitment/ E.B Exams" under "Our Services", and applications can be submitted online only. Online applications will be accepted from 9.00 a.m. on 26 October, 2026 until 9.00 p.m. on 20 November, 2026. This examination will be held only in Colombo in February 2027, subject to the rules and regulations prescribed by the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka. Qualifications :- 2.1 Educational Qualifications : I. Should have passed General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination with six (06) subjects including credit passes for Sinhala/ Tamil/ English language, Mathematic and two other subjects at one sitting. II. Should have passed General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) at least in one (01) subject (except the Common General Test and General English subjects). 2.2 Professional Qualifications : Should have obtained at least Level 3 of National Vocational Qualification (NVQ) with regard to the field of computer/ Information and communication Technology or an equivalent qualification of the above field recognized by the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission. 2.3 Experience : Should have 06 months experience in computer data entering. 2.4 Physical Qualifications : Every applicant should be physically and mentally fit to perform the duties of the post and ability to serve during day and night according to the service requirement. 2.5 Other Qualifications : I. Should be a citizen of Sri Lanka. II. Should be of excellent moral character. III. Should have fulfilled all the qualifications required to be recruited to the post as at the closing date for applications (20.11.2026). IV. No person holding priesthood of any religion is allowed to apply or appear for this competitive examination. 2.6 Age Limit : The candidate should be not less than 18 years and not more than 35 years of age as at closing date of applications.
