The qualifications required for recruitment to the post should have been fulfilled in every aspect as at the date specified in the notification for calling of applications. 2. Age limit : - Minimum age - 21 years - Maximum age - 32 years 3. Salary Scale : Rs. 40,500-10x450-10x490-7x540-15x590 - Rs. 62,530 Accordingly, the salary code applicable to this post is AC-PL-1-2025, and the initial salary step will be Rs. 40,500/.
