This is a multi-task service category where basic tasks that do not require specific semi-skilled or specialized proficiency are assigned, among the fundamental requirements for carrying out and maintaining the functions of institutions. According to the Service Minutes, the designated post for Grade III is Office Labourer. Any duty falling within this scope of functions shall be assigned to this service category by the Head of the Institution / Appointing Authority. Examples: Key duties, carrying letters and files, conducting cleaning activities as required, assisting in obtaining photocopies, handling fax messages, and any other official duties assigned by the institution. 06. Conditions of Service: I. Officers appointed must serve compulsorily for a minimum period of 03 years at their first place of appointment. If it becomes necessary to transfer any officer due to exceptional reasons determined by the Appointing Authority before the completion of the said period, the Appointing Authority shall have the power to do so. II. The conditions set out in the Procedural Rules published in the Extraordinary Gazette Notification of the Public Service Commission No. 2310/29 dated 14.12.2022, the provisions of the Establishment Code, and the contents of the Service Minutes of the Office Employees' Service shall also apply to this appointment. 07. Recruitment Qualifications: I. Must be a citizen of Sri Lanka. II. The age should be not less than 18 years and not more than 45 years as of the closing date for receiving applications. The upper age limit shall not apply to permanent and pensionable appointees already in the public service. III. Must possess an excellent character. IV. Every candidate must possess sufficient physical and mental fitness to serve in any part of the island and to discharge the duties of the post. V. Having passed the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination in not more than two sittings with six subjects, including at least two credit passes. VI. Must not be a person disqualified from being appointed to the public service in accordance with Chapter V of the Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission. VII. It is mandatory to have fulfilled all qualifications relevant to the post on or before the date calling for applications. Note : Since this recruitment is only for the performance of duties in the Office Employees' Service, it should be noted that possessing higher educational or professional qualifications does not entitle anyone to receive a different salary outside the Service Minutes of the Office Employees' Service without a formal procedure, or to be appointed to a post in such salary structure. 08. Method of Recruitment: Qualified applicants will be appointed to the post of Office Labourer, Grade III of the Office Employees' Service, strictly following the order of merit of the marks obtained by the applicants in a structured interview conducted by an interview board.
