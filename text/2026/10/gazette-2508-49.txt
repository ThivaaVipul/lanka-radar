Hewage Dhanushka Naleen Sampath Fernando, Returning Officer of the Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha, do hereby declare under section 10A(1)(a) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) that Mr. Wansekara Mudiyanselage Ruwan Pradeepa of the Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha has vacated his office of member of that Pradeshiya Sabha as he has ceased to be a member of the United National Alliance Party. H. D. N. Sampath Fernando, Returning Officer, Thumpane Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Kandy, 02nd October, 2026.
