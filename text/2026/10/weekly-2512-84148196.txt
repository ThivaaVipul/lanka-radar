(IIA) - GAZETTE OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA - 23.10.2026 HEALTH SERVICES COMMITTEE OF THE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION Ministry of Health and Mass Media Recruitment to the Post of Medical Officer (Primary Grade) in the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Medical Service - 2026 APPLICATIONS are invited from qualified candidates, by order of the Health Services Committee of the Public Service Commission, to fill 177 vacant positions in the Primary Grade of the Medical Officer Category in the Department of Ayurveda. This recruitment is conducted in accordance with Letter No. DMS/CAD/MOH/CM/Vol.V dated 23.09.2026 of the Director General of the Department of Management Services, and the Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Medical Service published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2199/10 dated 27.10.2020, along with its subsequent amendments. Applicants must fulfill the qualifications specified in this notice on or before 16th November, 2026. Applications prepared in accordance with the specimen form given at the end of this notice should be sent by Registerd Post to reach the "Commissioner General of Ayurvedic, Department of Ayurveda, Old Kottawa Road, Navinna, Maharagama" on or before the specified date. The top left-hand corner of the envelope must be clearly marked "Recruitment to the post of Medical Officer (Primary Grade) in the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Medical Service - 2026" Furthermore, Sinhalese applicants must write their name in English in addition to Sinhala, and Tamil applicants must write their name in English in addition to Tamil. Closing Date for Applications : 16th November, 2026. Note : Complaints regarding applications or related documents lost or delayed in the post will not be considered. Applicants must bear the loss/ disadvantage caused by delaying applications until the deadline. 01. Scheme of Recruitment : Graduates holding an Ayurveda/ Siddha/ Unani degree from a university recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) will be recruited based on the order of the merit list issued by the UGC for the respective years. In cases where graduates from several years apply, recruitment will be conducted by a General Interview Board appointed by the Appointing Authority, giving priority according to the order in the merit list of each respective year. 02. Grade of Recruitment : Primary Grade of the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Medical Service. 03. Conditions of Service : I. This post is pensionable. You shall be subject to any future policy decisions take by the Government regarding the pension scheme applicable to you. II. Officers appointed to this post will be subject to a probation period of three (03) years. Promotions after recruitment to Primary Grade posts will be made in accordance with the terms and conditions outlined in the Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic Medical Service. III. New appointees will be placed in vacant service locations based on their order of merit. Officers who assume duties under their initial appointment must serve a mandatory minimum period of two (02 years at their initial workstation. Transfers should not be requested during this period, nor will transfer be granted during this period. IV. This appointment is subject to the Procedural Rules of the Public Service Commission, the Establishment Code of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, the provisions and conditions of the Service Minute of the Sri Lanka Ayurvedic medical Service published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 2199/10 dated 27th October, 2020, Financial Regulations of the Government, and other departmental regulations. 04. Salary Scale : The monthly salary scale applicable to this post is : Rs. 91,750 - 3 x 2,400 - 7 x 2,420 - 2 x 2,490 - 16 x 3,900 - Rs. 184,170/- (SL-2-2025) 05.
