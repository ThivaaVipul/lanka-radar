Gurusingha, Returning Officer of the Polonnaruwa Municipal Council, acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mr. Warnakulasooriya Ranjith Lakshman Thamel was elected as the member of the Polonnaruwa Municipal Council. Manoji T. Gurusingha, Returning Officer, Polonnaruwa Municipal Council. District Elections Office, Polonnaruwa, 23rd October, 2026.
