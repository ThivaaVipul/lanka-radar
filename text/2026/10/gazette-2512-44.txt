Thevaruban Jenuka the member belonged, was required by me under Section 66A(1) of the aforesaid ordinance, to decide upon a candidate of the Porathivupattu Pradeshiya Sabha who belongs to such party and who is not disqualified under Section 9 of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) and who shall be declared Elected as a member within a period of thirty (30) days; And whereas the Secretary of the aforesaid recognized political party has communicated the decision to nominate Mrs. Anpalakan Kopika to fill the said vacancy; Therefore I, Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer of the Porathivupattu Pradeshiya Sabha acting in terms of Section 66A(1)(b) of the Local Authorities Elections Ordinance (Chapter 262) do hereby declare that Mrs. Anpalakan Kopika was elected as the member of the Porathivupattu Pradeshiya Sabha. Nagalingam Regan, Returning Officer, Porathivupattu Pradeshiya Sabha. District Elections Office, Batticaloa, 30th of October, 2026.
