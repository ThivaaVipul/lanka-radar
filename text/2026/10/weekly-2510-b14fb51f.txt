Educational Qualifications relevant to the Post- Applicants should have completed: a) B.Sc. (Nursing) Degree awarded by a foreign or state university recognized by the University Grants Commission; or b) B.Sc. (Nursing) Degree awarded by a university established by an Act of Parliament of Sri Lanka; or c) B.Sc. (Nursing) Degree recognized under Section 25A of the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978. 03. Physical Fitness and Other Qualifications- a) Every candidate shall be physically fit in all respects to serve in any part of Sri Lanka. b) Shall be a citizen of Sri Lanka c) Shall be of an excellent character.
