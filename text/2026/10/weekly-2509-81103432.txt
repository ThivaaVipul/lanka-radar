Qualifications: The following qualifications are applicable for recruitment to the post of Assistant Hostel Warden Grade III :- (a) Must be a citizen of Sri Lanka; (b) Must be not less than 25 years and not more than 35 years of age on the closing date for applications; (c) Must be of excellent character; (d) Every candidate must possess sufficient physical and mental fitness to serve in any part of Sri Lanka and to perform the duties of the post; (e) Must have fulfilled the following educational qualifications: Passing at least one (01) subject (excluding the Common General Test and General English) at the General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) Examination; AND Passing the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination in six (06) subjects at one and the same sitting, with credit passes for Sinhala/Tamil/English language, Mathematics, and two other subjects. Note - I. The language in which the applicant must have obtained a credit pass at the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) Examination shall be the language medium in which the applicant sits for this competitive examination. The final decision on the acceptance of qualifications obtained at the General Certificate of Education (Ordinary Level) and General Certificate of Education (Advanced Level) Examinations, or any other qualification obtained by sitting for an examination conducted by local or foreign educational institutions for the purpose of awarding the appointment, will be taken by the Secretary of the Ministry after confirmation from the relevant institutions and the Commissioner General of Examinations.
