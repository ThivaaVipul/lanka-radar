The Agreement is made and entered into between the Company and the Union by way of a final and conclusive settlement of the demands made by the Union on 11th Dec. 2025, for the duration of the Agreement. The Agreement shall be binding on the Company, Union and members of the Union who undertake not to seek the variation of any terms, conditions, or benefits during the effective period of this Agreement. 2. The Agreement shall be effective from 1st day of October 2025 to the 31st day of December 2028. The Agreement may be terminated by either party with one months’ notice in writing to the other, provided however, that no such written notice can be given by parties prior to 1st April 2028. Any notice of termination given prior to 1st April 2028 shall have no effect whatsoever. 3. The Agreement shall cover and bind all non-executive staff i.e., Manual, Supervisory & Clerical staff, employed on monthly contracts of employment at Haleon Lanka Enterprise Limited, save and except all Representatives in Coordinators in the Company. 4. The Union, the Employer and the Employees covered and bound by this Agreement undertake that they shall not during the continuance of this agreement seek to vary or change, in any manner, any of the terms and conditions agreed upon herein, except by mutual Agreement. 18. In the event of a dispute or grievance during the period of this Agreement, the Union shall in the first instance notify the Company in writing of any such dispute or grievance. If the Company has not settled such dispute or grievance within 10 working days from the date of receipt of Notice, the Union shall then be entitled to request a meeting for discussion/negotiation towards settling such dispute or grievance. If even after three such meetings (such three meetings to be concluded within 30 calendar days), there is no amicable settlement, the parties shall seek guidance and facilitation from the Employer’s Federation of Ceylon (EFC) and if such discussions would not lead to an amicable settlement thereafter the Union shall be free to consider reporting the matter to the Labour Department for conciliation. 19. The Union also agrees that its members will not resort to any Trade Union or collective action in order to secure the change of any terms and conditions of employment or benefits during the period of this Agreement. 20. All disputes between the Union and the Employer or between the Employer and Employees shall be settled in accordance with the provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act and the regulations made there under. 21. Any party to this Agreement shall not instigate, support, or engage in any unfair labour practices during the period of this Agreement. 22. Attendance and Shift Timeliness 24.1. All employees shall report to duty in accordance with the official working hours, with operations commencing at 8:00 AM and concluding as per the established schedule. Employees are strictly required to comply with assigned shift timings. Where pre- operational duties are required, employees so designated shall report at the times specified by their respective Department Managers. 24.2. Failure to adhere to prescribed attendance and shift requirements, including late reporting or unauthorized absence, may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company policies. 24.3 Employees shall, as a condition of employment, demonstrate flexibility and cooperation to ensure uninterrupted operational continuity. This includes, but is not limited to, undertaking extended working hours, if essential night shifts, inter-departmental assignments, multi-skilled functions, and providing coverage during the absence of other employees, as reasonably directed by management. 24.4. Employees are bound by such obligations which are deemed essential for the achievement of the Site Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and the maintenance of overall factory Operational Equipment Effectiveness (OEE). Non-compliance with these requirements may be considered a breach of duty. 25. Mid-Year Performance Rating The mid-year performance rating is subject to revision at management’s discretion based on the employees’ performance during the initial six (6) month period. Failure to demonstrate improvement in punctuality (including late attendance), safety compliance, and quality compliance may result in a downward revision of the final rating, while demonstrated improvement may result in an upward revision. 26. Production incentive scheme Management intends to introduce a production incentive scheme within the Manufacturing Department. All employees, including those in supporting functions, are expected to extend their full cooperation and actively contribute to the smooth implementation and ongoing effectiveness of this initiative. This collective support is essential to ensure the successful achievement of operational objectives and overall organizational performance. 27.
