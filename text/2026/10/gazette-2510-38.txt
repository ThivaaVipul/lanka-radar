Dr. P. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chairman of the Governing Board and the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka. The public is hereby informed that, due to the amalgamation of Associated Motor Finance Company PLC with LB Finance PLC, the Governing Board of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, in terms of the Finance Buseness Act, No. 42 of 2011, has cancelled the Licence issued to Associated Motor Finance Company PLC with effect from 31.07.2026.
